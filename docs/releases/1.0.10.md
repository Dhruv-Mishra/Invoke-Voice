# Invoke 1.0.10

## More Accurate, Faster Local Speech Recognition

- Make [NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v2](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2) the default local recognizer. It runs as a pinned, hash-verified INT8 ONNX export (about 660 MB) inside the existing speech worker, with the same voice activity detection, end-of-speech pause, push-to-talk and early decoding. It needs no new Python packages.
- Parakeet is English-only. Whisper Small stays available for other languages, and Moonshine Tiny stays opt-in. Saved recognizer choices are kept; choose **Parakeet** under **Settings > Local speech** and run setup to switch an existing install.
- `npm run models -- parakeet` downloads the model files from the command line, and `node scripts/bench-local.mjs stt parakeet` benchmarks the production worker.

## Smoother Local Speech

- Remove the dead air Kokoro adds around every phrase. Each phrase carried about 0.3 s of leading and 0.45 s of trailing silence, so every phrase boundary paused for about 0.75 s and the first word started 0.3 s late. Phrases now keep 20 ms before speech and a natural 300 ms pause after a sentence (120 ms after a clause).
- Speak later parts of an answer in whole sentences instead of 24 to 56 character fragments. Fragments were intoned as separate utterances, which, with the silence above, caused the pauses in the middle of sentences. The first phrase is unchanged so audio still starts quickly.

## Validation And Limits

Recognition accuracy, measured on 263 LibriSpeech clean/other and synthetic app-command clips (57 of them also mixed with pink noise at 10 dB SNR), on a Threadripper PRO 5955WX with eight recognition threads:

| Recognizer | WER clean | WER other | WER clean + noise | Median decode |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whisper Small INT8 (previous default) | 7.0% | 7.4% | 10.5% | 1.09 s |
| **Parakeet TDT 0.6B v2 INT8** | **3.0%** | **3.9%** | **4.6%** | **0.21 s** |

On identical subsets, Whisper small.en, distil-small.en, distil-medium.en, medium.en, distil-large-v3.5 and large-v3-turbo were all less accurate than Parakeet and took 0.9 s to 5.5 s per utterance, so none of them was adopted. Parakeet 0.6B v3 (multilingual) was less accurate on English than v2. Kokoro's INT8 ONNX export was four times slower than the current PyTorch model and its FP32 export was no faster, so Kokoro is unchanged apart from the silence trimming.

Through the production worker, push-to-talk release to final transcript fell from 1.04-1.44 s (Whisper) to 0.08-0.38 s. Hands-free recognition now finishes within about 0.2 s of the 1.4 s end-of-speech pause, compared with 1.50-2.55 s before.

In a six-turn synthetic voice test (end of speech to first audible word, median):

| Model | 1.0.9 | 1.0.10 |
|---|---|---|
| Gemma 4 E2B | 4.62 s | **3.36 s** |
| Qwen3.6 35B-A3B with MTP | 4.11 s | **3.29 s** |

Identical spoken answers now play 0.4 s to 2.6 s shorter because of the removed silence, and no playback gaps (synthesis falling behind playback) occurred in any run.

These are single-machine, synthetic measurements, not target-device guarantees. Physical microphones, accents, noisy rooms and laptop performance were not independently validated. Installers remain unsigned.
