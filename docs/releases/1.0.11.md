# Invoke 1.0.11

## Thinking Bubbles While The Assistant Works

- While a local voice turn is being generated, a short themed thought bubble grows out of the center sprite and says what stage the assistant is at: analyzing the request, taking action, checking results, then wrapping up. The line changes every 5 seconds, each stage has five different wordings, and every new bubble appears on a different side of the sprite without covering it. The bubble fades back into the sprite as soon as the real answer starts.
- Each line is also spoken in the theme's voice. A spoken line always finishes before the answer plays, and a new line is only spoken when nothing else is waiting to play, so lines never pile up ahead of the answer.
- The speech for these lines is synthesized once per voice and saved under the app's local data folder (`cache/thinking-speech`). Later turns and later app launches replay the saved audio without running Kokoro, so it uses no extra CPU while the model is generating.
- Applies to local voice calls (local and hybrid pipelines). Native hosted realtime voices are unchanged. Reduced motion and transparency settings are respected.

## Validation And Limits

Measured on a Threadripper PRO 5955WX with eight Kokoro threads, the 20 lines take 0.29-0.43 s each to synthesize the first time and play for 1.07-1.67 s. Generation itself is not slowed: once a line is saved it costs no model or speech work. An answer that becomes ready while a line is still playing waits for the rest of that line, at most about 1.6 s. In 1.0.10's synthetic test, the first Qwen answer audio was ready about 1.7 s after the end-of-speech pause, roughly when the first line ends, so this wait is usually short. It was not re-measured end to end for this release.

Tests and screenshots at 1440x900, 900x500 and 390x844 confirm the bubble stays outside the sprite. Physical audio playback on target devices was not independently validated. Installers remain unsigned.
