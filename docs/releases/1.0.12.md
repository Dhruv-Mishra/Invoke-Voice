# Invoke 1.0.12

## Thinking Lines Only When A Turn Is Slow

- Thinking bubbles and their spoken lines now wait 3 seconds before appearing. Answers that are ready sooner play straight away, without a bubble or filler speech in front of them. Slower turns show the first line at 3 seconds, then change it every 5 seconds as before.

## Captions Follow The Voice

- In local voice calls (local and hybrid pipelines), the assistant caption now shows each phrase when its audio starts playing, instead of showing the whole reply as soon as it is generated. The full reply still appears in the conversation log immediately.
- Caption updates now arrive once per spoken phrase instead of once per generated token, so the caption does less work during a reply.
- Native hosted realtime voices are unchanged.

## Refreshed Caption Cards

- Caption cards have softer 16 px corners, a thin accent line in the speaker's colour, a small three-bar voice indicator that moves while you are talking or while the assistant is speaking, and newly added words that fade in.
- Long captions keep the newest line in view, and older lines fade out at the top, so they no longer stay stuck on their first lines. Reduced motion and transparency settings are respected.

## Validation And Limits

Unit, provider and Electron browser tests cover the thinking delay, phrase captions and caption layout at 1440x960, 820x900, 390x844, 320x640 and 900x500. Captions start when a phrase begins playing and do not highlight individual words inside a phrase. Physical audio playback on target devices was not independently validated. Installers remain unsigned.
